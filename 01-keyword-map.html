  1. What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?From Google's AI Overview + PAA on “generative engine optimization.” Core definitional H2 — answer in 2 sentences, then expand.
  2. Is GEO replacing SEO?PAA on the primary term. Frame as complementary, not a replacement — matches how top-ranking pages (Coursera, HubSpot) answer it.
